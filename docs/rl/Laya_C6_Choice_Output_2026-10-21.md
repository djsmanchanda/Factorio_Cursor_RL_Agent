# Laya C6 choice and output

User intent, 2026-10-21: C6 is reached when the mall chooses to make advanced circuits and advanced circuits are actually made. The ops endpoint is `laya-c6-choice-output-v2`; it requires neither a sustained rate nor an advanced-circuit consumer. Geometry rate proxies and deterministic `c6-v1` retain their definitions.

An explicit `produce`, `mall_slot` or `grow` choice for `advanced-circuit` opens a receipt. A fresh engine item-production baseline is sampled after selection and immediately before execution. Positive subsequent item production on the same force and surface proves output; stock, a successful submission and entity lifetime craft counts do not. A wait preserves the receipt; another explicit mall-product choice supersedes a pending receipt. Slow progress has no additional expiry beyond the bounded run. A completed receipt remains an achievement after a loan restores its original recipe.

Attribution uses the complete observed recipe catalog, compatible machine prototypes and full-surface infrastructure counts. Other recipes producing advanced circuits must have no compatible machine present at baseline or subsequent observations. This supports the actual Space Age catalog, whose recycling recipes otherwise make an unconditional sole-recipe test unusable. Missing capability, a compatible alternate machine, changed scope/catalog, failed counter reads or regressing ticks/counters make the interval unknown. A new explicit choice is required to open another interval. The contract applies only to an isolated automated disposable run with no concurrent player or script item production; it does not identify the particular producing assembler or item genealogy.

Evidence is retained separately in `summary.laya_acceptance.C6`, latest status and `laya_acceptance` journal events, with version, run, choice, baseline/current ticks, recipe/catalog/mod signature, counter source, units and item delta. The existing `accepted`/`accepted_final` fields continue to report deterministic predicates. Reports must name both endpoints separately. Receipts are run-local: restart/jumpstart does not import an old choice as new evidence.

Historical C6 counts are not comparable to this endpoint and must not be relabelled. The electric-furnace consumer option in `d491d6c` is excluded from the takeover branch: it targeted the previous consumer requirement. Furnace-stock legality and transitive seed sizing remain independent runtime fixes, to be evaluated in matched arms.

Validation: focused choice/output and runner tests, plus direct normalization of the recorded C11 `copper-s79405` initial observation (662 recipes, 41 alternate recycling recipes, no compatible recycler). These are offline evidence; a fresh frozen episode is required for live evidence. No installed Lua mod changes are needed.
