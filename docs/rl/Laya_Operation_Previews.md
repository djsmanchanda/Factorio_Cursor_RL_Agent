# Operation previews

Version 1 previews accompany produce and production-service menu options and
selected decision/training records. They expose the requested final stock,
observed loan identity and lender, known reservation owner, current step and
craft progress, and grounded dependency edges. Unknown facts remain explicit.
An inferred loan claimant alone does not establish an active material project.

For one observed active loan, the economics observation samples stock,
transferable final output, and craft progress during a recorded tick interval.
The shared execution calculation applies prerequisite credits, target selection,
and persisted-step protection to propose a recipe, target, and craft count.
The proposal records its stage as before admission. Live preemption, chemical
admission, release, ownership and freshness checks remain authoritative; the
proposal grants no execution or release authority. It does not predict all
branches of a produce or production-service operation.

The ingredient bill is gross recipe input for the proposed configured step,
without requester headroom. It is not the construction bill, the local delivery
shortfall, or a new reservation. The preview allocator executes the shared
allocation algorithm on an isolated projection; evaluating prospective options
cannot rebalance the live ledger or acquire holds. A missing stock/progress
sample, clock regression or changing ledger leaves the proposal unknown.
The recorded interval is sampled evidence, not an atomic game snapshot.

The menu projection makes no network calls and changes no selection weights.
Dependency edges do not prove a closed deadlock or authorize a cycle breaker.
No prospective lender is chosen and no current loan is interrupted by preview.
Mid-loan restart equivalence, full admission prediction, cycle escape and live
acceptance remain open. Future use requires a matching frozen Python runner;
no mod deployment is needed for this Python-only boundary.
