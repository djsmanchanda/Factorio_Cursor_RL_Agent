# Operation evidence and accounting

The version 1 envelope describes an operation: unknown, deferred, prerequisite advanced, submitted, constructing, delivered, producing, completed or fault. A declared `done` requires explicit `ok=true`; observed construction requires integer zero pending/missing counts. Contradictory completion and absent evidence stay unknown. Enclosed typed evidence is revalidated against raw fields, operation identity and scope. A producing cell, delivered construction and a completed mission are separate results.

`reconcile_records` is the shared runner-summary and controller boundary. It retains one slot per recorded decision, collapses exact duplicate run/index rows and makes conflicting duplicates unknown. Malformed rows occupy unknown slots. It joins a transaction only by the actual recorded transaction filename and matching receipt/option identity; an operation observation requires the recorded invocation ID. Anonymous historical receipts stay uncorrelated. Initial state counts remain alongside reconciled state counts, so a later delivery cannot hide the original fault population. These counts describe available evidence; a lost journal cannot establish zero attempts.

`operation_attempts` counts selected nonwait, nonplanning operations. `objective_attempts` groups these by the recorded phase and need, including prerequisites; it is not proof of attempting the final requested milestone directly. Unknown option identities have an explicit count. The job objective result comes from its separately measured target. Process completion, caught exceptions, typed faults, operation completion and target completion retain separate fields.

Every scheduler publication with an immutable planned-job file includes `all_planned_jobs` accounting. Cancelled, failed, undispatched and missing status rows remain in the denominator; absent attempt or objective evidence stays unknown. New milestone-contract rows use observed transition provenance for earned targets and expose inherited eligibility; legacy current-pass scoring remains explicitly labelled. Neither unknown historical identity nor a missing final observation becomes a fault or a success.

Runner and collector summaries use the same reconciliation function. New asynchronous PM observations must carry the actual selected invocation ID and measured postcondition; construction of a prerequisite cannot complete its caller's economic objective. Track B receipts keep their actual transaction identity. This contract establishes source/fixture evidence only. Future use requires matching frozen runner/controller copies; no live result or policy adoption follows from the counters.
