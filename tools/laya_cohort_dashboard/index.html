Performance & admission

p95 and p98 are lower-tail coverage floors: the 5th and 2nd percentiles of sampled run UPS, not high quantiles.

Missing samples are unknown, not zero. History is a bounded tail; gaps are not attributed. Scheduler reasons may overlap and are not causal proof.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES

Read before comparing

Ever and final are distinct snapshots. Strict C6 v1, ops-Laya v2 and candidate v3 are different predicates. Missing predicate versions and comparison eligibility stay unknown. Partial cohorts are not final rankings; attempts and retries are separate. A zero failure counter does not mean zero typed tool faults. Notes are user observations, never learning truth.

Run ports come from world manifests, not a verified network route. A UDP listener, matching Factorio version and matching mods are required. An SSH TCP tunnel does not forward Factorio UDP.
