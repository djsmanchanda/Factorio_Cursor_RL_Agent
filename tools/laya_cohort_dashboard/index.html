What limits more concurrent runs?

? How to read these limits
  • Limits count concurrent runs, not cohorts. The ceiling is an upper bound; safeguards can admit fewer.
  • Slow-tail UPS (p95 / p98 coverage). Every sample ranks the running worlds by UPS. p95 coverage is the speed 95% of runs meet or exceed (the 5th percentile, the slow tail); p98 is the same at 98% (2nd percentile). They are not high percentiles.
  • Growth threshold. One more run is tried only when p95 coverage clears its floor plus a margin and temperature is below the target minus a recovery margin.
  • Upper limit = the lower of the manual ceiling (if any) and the automatic cap for the mode. Older samples may omit the manual ceiling.
  • Flags compare the latest sample with thresholds. They show proximity to a limit, not proven cause; the scheduler’s own recorded reason is shown separately.

Reading rules

  • Strict and proxy are different families. A proxy phase can be reached while strict checkpoints fail; never read them together.
  • Predicate versions differ. Strict C6 v1, ops-Laya v2 and candidate v3 are different predicates. A missing version or eligibility is shown as unknown, never as a pass.
  • Ever and final are distinct snapshots. Early finals may be latched and are then unknown. Pending, invalid and no-start rows are unranked. Unknown is null, not zero.
  • No pooling. Cohorts differ in seeds, engine, tick windows and host; they are not matched replications or a trend. Attempts and retries are separate.
  • Counters are partial. A zero failure counter does not mean zero typed tool faults. Notes are user observations, never learning truth.
  • Run ports come from world manifests, not a verified network route. A UDP listener, matching Factorio version and matching mods are required. An SSH TCP tunnel does not forward Factorio UDP.
  • Local-only dashboard: no RCON credentials, no automatic joins or syncs. Historical index: docs/rl/Laya_Cohort_Results_Index.md
